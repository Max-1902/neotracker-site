Privacy Policy

How the NEO Tracker iPhone app and this website handle your data.

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Deutsch

Summary

NEO Tracker has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not collect or receive any data about you.

Your Location

With your permission, your location is used on your device to compute the sky above you and when objects rise and set. To show a place name and time zone, your coordinates (and any place you type in the location search) are sent to Apple's maps service; see Apple's privacy policy. Your location is never sent to the developer or to any data provider below.

Data Providers

To show live data, the app downloads public information from these services. The requests contain only what's needed to look up the data (for example an asteroid's designation, a date range or the name of a star) and, like any internet request, your device's IP address:

  • NASA NeoWs (api.nasa.gov): weekly close approaches
  • NASA Science (science.nasa.gov): Astronomy Picture of the Day
  • NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics (ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov, ssd.jpl.nasa.gov): orbits, impact monitoring, close approaches, new discoveries, fireballs, comet orbits and asteroid positions (Horizons)
  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons: object summaries and pictures
  • Spaceflight News API: news articles
  • NASA (www.nasa.gov, science.nasa.gov): full text of NASA news articles
  • NASA Eyes on Asteroids and Eyes on the Solar System: NASA web pages shown inside the app

Articles from other news sites open in Safari inside the app, which loads the publisher's own page.

Each provider's own privacy policy applies to its servers.

Stored on Your Device

Settings, favorites, quiz progress, your observing log, saved locations and cached data and images are stored only on your device. Clear the cache in Settings; deleting the app removes everything.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, alerts are scheduled locally on your device. No push service or server is involved.

Not Affiliated with NASA

NEO Tracker is an independent educational app. It uses public data from NASA, JPL and other sources but is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or JPL.

This Website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when it delivers the pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.
