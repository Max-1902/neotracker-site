Support

Help with the NEO Tracker iPhone app.

Contact

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Is any of this an official warning?

No. The risk score is a simplified, educational indicator, not an official hazard rating. For real impact assessments, rely on NASA's CNEOS and ESA's NEO Coordination Centre.

Where does the data come from?

From public services: NASA NeoWs and APOD, the NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics APIs, Wikipedia and the Spaceflight News API. Each data provider is listed in the privacy policy.

Why does a feed say it couldn't load?

The live data needs an internet connection, and providers sometimes limit how often they can be asked. The app shows the last data it loaded; pull down to try again later.

Can I read whole news articles in the app?

Articles by NASA open in full inside the app. Articles from other news sites open on the publisher's own website, in Safari inside the app.

Does the app collect data about me?

No. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.

NEO Tracker is an independent educational app, not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or JPL.
